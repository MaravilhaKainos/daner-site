About Daner

Experience built in the gas sector. A brand ready to evolve.

Daner Energias is an Angolan company specialised in gas solutions, built on years of technical experience, knowledge of the sector and commitment to its customers.

Our story

The experience stays. The brand evolves.

Daner Energias is the evolution of Gás Repair. The change of name and identity reflects the company's growth and its ambition to follow new opportunities in the energy sector.

We keep the same team, technical experience and commitment to our customers. We have evolved our brand to better represent who we are today and the path we want to keep building.

Mission

To develop and secure safe, efficient energy solutions for the industrial, commercial and residential sectors, with technical knowledge and a close relationship with customers.

Vision
