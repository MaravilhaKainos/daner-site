Equipment conversion

We assess your equipment and, if viable, adapt it to run on LPG. Viability depends on the characteristics of each piece of equipment.

What we do

Equipment conversion

Technical assessment

We analyse the equipment, the fuel it uses today and the site conditions.

Equipment suitability

We check whether it can be adapted and which components are needed.

Conversion and installation

We adapt the equipment and connect it to the LPG network or tank.

Testing and start-up

We test the equipment before it goes to work.

Where it applies

Where it applies

Gas burns cleaner and tends to wear equipment less than liquid fuels. The actual gain depends on each case and we only confirm it after the assessment.

  • Industrial kilns, as in ceramics, metallurgy and glass
  • Boilers
  • Machines that run on diesel or other fuels

Tell us the equipment and the fuel you use today. Our technical team will assess it and reply.
