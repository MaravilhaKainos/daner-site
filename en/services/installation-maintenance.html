What we do

Installation and maintenance

Network installation

Execution of the network by qualified technicians, to the design and technical standards.

Testing and checks

Leak-tightness test before the network enters service.

Documentation and compliance

Certificate of Conformity and inspection by the competent authorities, under Executive Decree no. 83/15.

Maintenance

Preventive and corrective maintenance, to keep the installation safe and running.

Where it applies

Two solutions, depending on consumption

The solution depends on consumption and available space. We help you choose.

Network with cylinders

For lower consumption, such as houses, small restaurants and offices. A protected cylinder room and a network to the appliances.

Network with tanks

For higher consumption, such as housing complexes, hotels, industrial kitchens and factories.
