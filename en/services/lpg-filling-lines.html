LPG filling line assembly

Complete lines for filling LPG cylinders, with control and safety.

We design and assemble LPG cylinder filling lines, from study to start-up. The scope includes the carousel, conveyors, weight and leak control and the supporting networks.

What we do

LPG filling lines

Filling carousel

Electronic carousel with up to 18 stations, with filling machines with scales.

Conveyors

Chain conveyors and mechanisms for cylinder entry and exit.

Control and safety

Weight checks, leak detection, valve sealing and removal of defective cylinders.

Gas and utilities

Compressors, pumps, LPG tanks and gas, compressed air and fire networks.

Where it applies

Who it is for, and how safely

Aimed at LPG distributors and industrial plants that fill cylinders.

  • Lines for 10 to 15 kg and 35 to 50 kg cylinders
  • Capacity sized to your filling volume
  • Ventilation under the carousel, automatic gas shut-off in a fire and flameproof equipment
Experience

Capacity and method

450 to 1,800cylinders per hour, depending on the carousel
up to 18filling stations per carousel

Reference values for a typical project. Final capacity depends on the number of stations and the cylinder type. The equipment is designed to the applicable European directives.

In three stages

  1. Study and sizingCapacity, cylinder type and available space.
  2. Assembly and installationLine, networks and electrical connections for explosive atmospheres.
  3. Testing and start-upTests, scale calibration and supervised start-up, with operator training.
